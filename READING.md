# Reading list for future revisions of *Sub ek*

Papers to read one at a time, both to refine the whitepaper and to learn from the times and people who wrote them. Moved out of the paper's References in v1.1.

- [ ] **1945**: Vannevar Bush, "As We May Think," *The Atlantic*. Associative trails; memory of contact. *(Citation not yet checked.)*
- [ ] **1960**: J. C. R. Licklider, "Man-Computer Symbiosis," *IRE Transactions on Human Factors in Electronics*. Partnership rather than tool or companion. *(Citation not yet checked.)*
- [ ] **1998**: Andy Clark & David Chalmers, "The Extended Mind," *Analysis*. The substrate as part of the mind. *(Citation not yet checked.)*
- [ ] **2019**: Martin Kleppmann, Adam Wiggins, Peter van Hardenberg & Mark McGranaghan, "Local-first software: you own your data, in spite of the cloud," *Onward! 2019*, pp. 154–178. https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3359591.3359737. A profile its people own.
- [ ] **2023**: Charles Packer et al., "MemGPT: Towards LLMs as Operating Systems," arXiv:2310.08560. https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.08560. Lazy traversal as paging.
